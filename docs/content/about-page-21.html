ZEUS Cabinets & Countertops is a family-owned business designing and building kitchen and bathroom cabinetry and countertops for homeowners across Orlando and Central Florida — in-stock collections for fast turnaround, and custom cabinetry for spaces that need an individual solution.

How We Work

We start every project with a conversation about how you actually use your space. Most kitchens and bathrooms move fastest with an in-stock collection; some spaces — closets, built-ins, unusual dimensions — need custom cabinetry instead. Design, cabinetry, and countertops are planned and coordinated together by ZEUS.

All standard cabinetry colors and finishes are available in stock through our central warehouse. The only exceptions are Hyper Colors and Euro / Flat Panel.

What We Build

In-stock and custom kitchen and bathroom cabinetry, countertops in quartz, granite, porcelain, and marble, and custom storage for closets, laundry rooms, pantries, and home offices.

Where We Work

ZEUS is a service-area business serving Orlando, Windermere, Winter Garden, Horizon West, Clermont, and Dr. Phillips. We meet with you for a consultation rather than operating a public walk-in showroom.
